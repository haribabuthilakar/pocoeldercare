| External vs Internal JWT Payloads | Unauthenticated or untrusted callers must not access internal JWT contracts or Ops-specific role payloads. |

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-01-01 | Elevation of Privilege | packages/types/src/auth.ts | high | mitigate | Distinct tagged `ExternalJwtPayload` and `InternalJwtPayload` types with separate `tokenType` discriminators preventing token cross-use (D-113, D-114). |
| T-01-02 | Information Disclosure | packages/eslint-config | medium | mitigate | ESLint `no-restricted-paths` boundary guards preventing backend code or secrets from leaking into frontend/UI bundles (D-16). |
</threat_model>
