<threat_model>
ASVS Level: 1
Threats Considered:
- **SEC-01 (Session Hijacking & Insecure Storage):** Unauthorized device access in the field exposing senior medical and personal data.
  - Mitigation Task: Task 1 persists JWT tokens inside hardware-backed SecureStore, adds a 4-digit PIN unlock screen with automatic timeout locking after backgrounding, and provides instant session clearing on logout.
- **SEC-02 (Offline Mutation Forgery & Tampering):** Malformed or tampered local mutation payloads bypassing server validation.
  - Mitigation Task: Task 2 and Task 3 sign client mutations with client-generated UUIDs, enforce typed Zod mutation payloads, and submit them to `POST /api/field/v1/sync/batch` where NestJS `@poco/business-rules` execute server-authoritative state checks before committing to PostgreSQL.
</threat_model>
