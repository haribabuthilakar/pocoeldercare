Purpose: Empower Care Officer Managers to maintain 1:1 officer allocations under strict compliance standards and enable Operations Managers to manage versioned service catalogs without breaking existing customer billing terms.
Output: Production-ready Care Officer roster and Service Catalog management interfaces with comprehensive component test coverage.

<threat_model>
ASVS Level: 1
Threats Considered:
- **SEC-02 (Unauthorized Manager Override):** Non-manager user attempting to assign an uncertified officer by tampering with the client request.
  - Mitigation Task: Task 1 and Task 2 verify caller role permissions (`CARE_MANAGER` or `SUPER_ADMIN`) before unlocking the override toggle; backend validates role and writes mandatory `AuditLog` entry.
- **SEC-03 (Rate Card Tampering / Price Corruption):** Negative prices or float rounding errors during catalog version updates.
  - Mitigation Task: Task 3 uses `createServiceCatalogVersionSchema` to strictly enforce positive integer paise (`pricePaise = priceRupees * 100`) and immutable version creation.
</threat_model>
