<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Database Connection & Queries | All runtime queries pass through the typed PrismaClient singleton. |
| Two-Root Auth Database Boundary | External Person credentials/roles are strictly partitioned from InternalUser staff credentials. |
| Financial Ledger Immutability | WalletTransaction records are append-only; balance cannot be updated without a recorded transaction. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-SC | Tampering | npm dependencies (@prisma/client, prisma) | high | mitigate | Synchronized Prisma 6 dependency catalog versions and locked pnpm-lock.yaml. |
| T-01-03 | Elevation of Privilege | packages/database/prisma/schema/auth.prisma | critical | mitigate | Database-level schema partition between Person (family/seniors) and InternalUser (staff) preventing role mixing (D-29, AUTH-01, AUTH-02). |
| T-01-04 | Tampering | packages/database/prisma/schema/billing.prisma | high | mitigate | Restrict onDelete constraints on WalletTransaction and immutable audit ledger preventing transaction manipulation (D-30, D-45, BILL-01). |
| T-01-05 | Information Disclosure | packages/database/prisma/schema/household.prisma | high | mitigate | SeniorMedicalProfile separated as a 1:1 relation from generic Senior demographics for strict PII/clinical data control (D-35). |
</threat_model>
